# Pre-deployment checklist

Complete and sign this checklist before programming a study batch.

## Protocol

- [ ] Research question and primary accelerometer outcomes are documented.
- [ ] Device model, firmware, placement, orientation, and wear schedule are approved.
- [ ] Sampling frequency, recording start/stop schedule, and time zone are approved.
- [ ] Non-wear, valid-day, minimum-day, and weekend-coverage rules are pre-specified.
- [ ] Sleep-log requirements and fallback rules are pre-specified.
- [ ] The study has a plan for daylight-saving-time transitions and travel.

## Devices and identifiers

- [ ] Device serial numbers have been reconciled against the inventory.
- [ ] Batteries are charged and device clocks are checked.
- [ ] A coded participant ID format is defined; personal names will not be used in filenames.
- [ ] The deployment log links coded ID, device ID, batch, operator, and scheduled dates.
- [ ] A second person or automated check verifies each device-to-ID assignment.

## Participant-facing materials

- [ ] Wear, removal, charging, bathing, sleep, and contact instructions are approved.
- [ ] Correct placement, orientation, and strap/adhesive fit are demonstrated.
- [ ] Return packaging, return date, and troubleshooting contact are confirmed.
- [ ] Consent and privacy procedures cover device data and accompanying logs.

## Data management

- [ ] Access-controlled raw, converted, processed, QC, clean, and final folders exist.
- [ ] Raw downloads will be treated as read-only after verification.
- [ ] Download, conversion, QC, cleaning, and exclusion logs are ready.
- [ ] Backup and retention procedures are documented.
- [ ] One end-to-end pilot file has been programmed, downloaded, processed, and reviewed.
