Interpret with care

What the dashboard counts

Entered course
An authenticated learner whose enrollment event was accepted by the server.
First-attempt accuracy
Server-scored correct answers on each learner's first attempt divided by first-attempt answers in scope.
Module 8 complete
A server-accepted completion record for Module 8—not merely a page view.
Active certificate
A non-revoked, server-issued certificate record. Revoked records remain in the Certificates detail view.

Overview date filters select learners by their first accepted course-entry timestamp in UTC; later outcomes for that cohort remain included. Recent activity can take a few seconds to appear.
